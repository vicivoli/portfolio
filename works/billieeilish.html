Overview

Russian-language ad for the documentary

A commercial video created for the documentary “The World’s a Little Blurry,” translated into Russian and reinterpreted through motion design and kinetic typography.

This piece blends dynamic text, rhythmic cuts, and layered visual textures to mirror the emotional intensity of Billie Eilish’s story while adapting it for a Russian-speaking audience.
