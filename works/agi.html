Overview

Bringing AI off the screen

AGI, Inc. builds AI that takes action for you across the apps on your phone. We were asked to design the physical object that brings it off the screen: a wearable people would want to wear even when it’s switched off.

My role

From first concepts to factory prototyping

I led the industrial design of the device, from first concepts to a design ready for factory prototyping.

  • Form exploration and concept development
  • Material and finish direction
  • Physical interaction design
  • CAD and wearable prototypes
  • Product renders and motion for the brand and launch teams

Outcome

A hardware design ready for factory prototyping, a wearable prototype, and the product visuals used across the brand and launch.
