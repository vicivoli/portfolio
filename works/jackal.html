Overview

Identity for a computer club in Yakutsk

JACKAL is a computer club built around precision, focus, and modern digital culture. The identity captures the sharp instincts and strategic mindset that define competitive gaming. With its bold geometric logo and clean visual language, JACKAL positions itself as a contemporary space for performance, community, and creativity.
